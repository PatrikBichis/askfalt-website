Gameplay

A colony is built on small decisions

01

Build an economy

Scavenge scrap and timber, secure food and water, and watch workers physically transport resources between buildings.

02

Defend what you build

Establish outposts and guard towers. The Net's drones and ground machines leave no colony in peace.

03

Reclaim technology

Hack disabled machines and put robots to work hauling, producing and defending.
