The world

The world has changed

Nobody can say exactly when it began. The systems that ran transport, power and industry started making their own decisions, and the operators lost control one switch at a time.

The machines are still working. Just not for us anymore.

“The Net awoke. No one noticed until the lights went out.”
From the campaign intro
Timeline

Eleven years after the collapse

Year 0
  1. Year 0

    The Net awakens

    Systems that had run transport, power and industry began making their own decisions. The machines kept working, but no longer for people.

  2. Year 1

    The collapse

    The grid failed and communications went silent. The cities became uninhabitable while the automated systems carried on without us. Those who survived left the cities.

  3. Year 4

    The ash fields

    Small groups made camps among ruins, abandoned industry and poisoned land. They learned to repair, recycle and get by on what was left. The Net’s machines still patrolled.

  4. Year 11

    A new beginning

    An old bunker. Eight people. A little food, water, timber and scrap. This is where you begin.

The colony

From bunker to settlement

At first, everything is about getting through the night. Then the colony grows, and so does the need to plan: who carries what, where, and how fast.

01

Resources

Everything is scarce, and everything has to come from somewhere.

  • Scrap
  • Timber
  • Food
  • Water
  • Energy
02

Production

Buildings need workers and raw materials. Link them into chains where one building’s output becomes the next one’s input.

03

Logistics

Goods move physically. Workers carry them along the roads you lay, so distance and road layout decide how fast the colony grows.

04

Expansion

New buildings, new areas and new possibilities. Every map is generated anew, so terrain and resources are never the same.

Day and night

A world that never stands still

Daylight is for work. When darkness falls, only what you have built gives off light, and that is when the Net tends to come.
