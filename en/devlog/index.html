Development

Devlog

What is happening in Askfält, version by version. The game is developed in small steps. Web/PWA test versions are shown separately from stable versions and app builds.

DevlogRoadmapPlay the latest version
  1. In progressOct 26, 2026v2.9

    Playtest fixes and campaign progress

    • The development build has four reviewed playtest fixes: level 3 requires a completed workshop instead of a repair, an idle guard robot can relieve a human in a tower or outpost, the Map layers button gives quick display choices, and wrecks have clearer graphics. These fixes have not been published yet.
    • On one test map, level 11 and both paths in level 12 have been completed with normal and short days in separate reviewed cases using normal player actions. Saving, loading and restarting have been tested in a mobile viewport. Level 13 has now also been completed in a reviewed case on the test map, using the help path from level 12 and normal days, in just under 54 minutes. Level 4 has also been completed in a reviewed case with normal days in just over seven minutes, with three attacks repelled and no buildings lost. Level 5 has been completed in a separate reviewed case in just under a minute, with two original wrecks restored and a robot guarding the tower. Level 6 has been completed in a reviewed case in just under three minutes, meeting the trade and population goals through normal exchanges, housing and recruitment. Both cases use the test map and normal days. Level 8 has also been completed in just over seven minutes in a separate reviewed case on the test map, with all 14 original residents surviving and no buildings lost. Level 10 has also been completed in a separate reviewed case at the start of day 8, with all original residents surviving. Starting, saving, loading and restarting the expedition base have been tested internally. In a separate ten-minute test, the depot’s steel reached the bunker and the food and water chains continued through night and dawn. Starting, saving, loading and restarting have now also been tested internally on mobile and desktop, with both display modes. It is still not a menu option. Two separate twenty-minute supply tests on the test map preserved all eight people present at the start and every building. Kits were produced and installed, and an experienced worker produced steel that was delivered to the bunker. One test tries an extra water cost as an internal recipe experiment; this is not a change to the game rules. Broader campaign and balance checks remain.

    • The free-play menu is complete in the development branch, with New colony and Damaged workshop options, a map code and a save slot. The Only the Net checkbox disables the rival colony’s tribute and raids, as well as expeditions and infiltrators. The trading post still exchanges 5 wood for 2 parts, and the Net’s threats and normal events remain. The starting scenario, map, day length and Only the Net setting are preserved when saving, loading and restarting. The setting has been tested both on and off in a mobile viewport.

      The development candidate now also has attack frequency and starting stock options for New colony. Attack frequency can be Low, Normal or High and changes the intervals between the Net’s attack attempts. The time of the first attempt and enemy health and damage are unchanged. Starting stock can be Limited, Standard or Generous: 14/16/4, 20/24/8 or 28/32/12 scrap/wood/parts respectively. Other starting goods are unchanged. Both options can be combined with Only the Net. Damaged workshop uses Normal and Standard. Loading preserves the current stock; restarting restores the chosen starting stock. Game starts, saving, loading, restarting and the mobile menu have been tested. These options have not been published in the PWA.

      Saving and restarting have also been tested for the abandoned roads and besieged colony scenarios. In one food test, an extra farm met the food goal for a larger colony; food balance remains under review. The published PWA is still 2.7.0-beta.1 and the latest stable version is 2.3. The new starting options and Only the Net setting have not been published.

      An abandoned colony has also passed a supply test with wreck repair through a night. Separate water-chain tests have now also reached steel production and delivery to the bunker; overall balance remains under review.

      A besieged colony has now sustained its supply chains and tower defense in one 20-minute test. Lower and higher attack frequencies have also been tested; these options are not yet available in the PWA.

      Level 7 has now been completed in a separate reviewed test on the test map with normal days, in just over 30 minutes. Ten steel produced in the colony reached the bunker, all 13 original residents survived, and no buildings were lost. In level 9, a replacement farm has been built at the ordinary cost, staffed and used to deliver food to the bunker. In a separate reviewed test, the relay’s full construction cost was delivered, the relay was completed and transmission reached about 72% before the relay was lost. A complete playthrough of level 9 using normal player actions remains to be verified. For the final level’s plunder path, the inherited starting stock was previously verified through saving, loading and restarting. In one separately reviewed test covering an hour of game time, 13 humans remained at the end, four robot wrecks had been restored and the guard goal was met. Cleaning reached 8 of 40 tiles and the core remained. The test did not reach a natural victory. These changes have not been published.

    A newer test shows that level 9 still has unfinished goals: six units of steel produced in the colony reached the bunker, the relay was completed, and transmission reached about 57% before the relay was lost. The third solar panel was still missing. On level 13’s plunder path, the guard goal was met and four wrecks were restored, but cleaning stopped at 8 of 40 tiles and the core remained. A v2.9 TestFlight build is being prepared while campaign and balance work continues; it is not yet available to test there.

  2. Final checks completeOct 16, 2026v2.8

    Final checks of enemies, robots and salvage are complete

    • Five enemy roles have distinct shapes, and wrecks retain their type. The development build includes repairs into guards, more expensive reprogramming into general-purpose robots, and salvage materials that must be carried home.
    • The mobile panel shows wreck choices, cost, result and work in progress. Functional tests, balance comparisons and independent review of this development step are complete.
    • The v2.8 development work is now integrated. There has been no separate v2.8 release or version bump. The PWA version label remains 2.7.0-beta.1; this milestone does not update TestFlight or the App Store.
  3. MilestoneOct 15, 2026v2.7

    Final checks of the dynamic threat are complete

    Balance comparisons and separate performance checks have been completed and independently reviewed. The published web/PWA version remains 2.7.0-beta.1 and the latest stable version is 2.3. This milestone does not release a new version or update TestFlight or the App Store.

  4. PWA test versionOct 14, 20262.7.0-beta.1

    A PWA beta for testing on phones

    • Territory, defensive lines, gates, self-defence and the Net’s scout drones from v2.4–v2.6 are included.
    • V2.7’s dynamic threat and Net pressure panel are included for testing. Final checks were completed and independently reviewed on October 15, 2026. The published PWA version remains 2.7.0-beta.1.
    • This is a web/PWA test version. It does not update TestFlight or the App Store.
  5. Final checks completeOct 14, 2026v2.7

    Dynamic threat: final checks complete

    Attacks respond to time and colony development. Ground and air attacks can be coordinated, and the Net pressure panel explains the level without revealing hidden targets or exact attack times. Missions survive loading and old observations can mislead. These features are included in the web/PWA 2.7.0-beta.1; final checks and independent review are complete. The published PWA version is unchanged.

  6. Included in the PWA betaOct 14, 2026v2.6

    The Net’s intelligence

    The Net’s units choose targets from received observations, and old positions can be outdated. Scout drones must transmit their private packets; towers, air protection and EMP can stop them. The map layer and saving have been verified. These features are included in 2.7.0-beta.1; this development step had no separate version release.

  7. Included in the PWA betaOct 14, 2026v2.5

    Defence and combat

    Defensive lines and gates, adult self-defence and watchtowers targeting light drones are implemented. Combat has clearer bullet trails, hits and drone outcomes, and ongoing attacks survive loading. These features are included in 2.7.0-beta.1; this development step had no separate version release.

  8. Included in the PWA betaOct 13, 2026v2.4

    Territory and expansion

    The map view shows the colony’s border, what expands it and where defence is missing. Area, perimeter and clear symbols make expansion opportunities and exposed edges easier to understand. These features are included in 2.7.0-beta.1; this development step had no separate version release.

  9. BetaOct 11, 2026iPhone and iPad

    The iPhone app is in TestFlight

    Askfält is now an app for iPhone and iPad in Apple's TestFlight beta programme – most recently with version 2.3. It is the same game as on the web, packaged so that everything lives on the phone and works offline. The first builds are being tested internally on real phones: graphics, touch, sound, saving and resuming.

    • Sound starts when you lift your finger after the first tap and plays even when the ring/silent switch is set to silent.
    • The target is the App Store on November 1, 2026 – subject to testing and Apple's review.
  10. ReleasedOct 11, 2026v2.3

    Roads and logistics 2.0

    • Plan a road with a start, a goal and up to two waypoints, see the price before you order it, and upgrade chosen stretches straight from the map.
    • A new Logistics view: traffic, waiting goods, the cargo and routes of the trucks, and why something is stuck.
    • Road building shares carriers with ordinary deliveries, even when the colony has only one free carrier.
    • Days are twice as long – four minutes instead of two – leaving more time between nights. For the old pace, choose Day length: Short in Settings.
    • Carriers fetch from the nearest building that has the goods, instead of the one built first.
  11. ReleasedOct 11, 2026v2.2

    A stable foundation and an economy that can restart

    • The economy can restart even if the last lumber camp or workshop is gone – a free adult in the bunker can slowly gather and make parts by hand.
    • A loading screen on start, clearer error messages and a tent camp at the edge of the territory for survivors waiting for beds.
    • Better readability on small screens, clearer campaign texts and daily reports that can be turned off.
    • Sound starts more reliably on iPhone, and the game also runs in browsers without WebGL using simpler fallback graphics.
  12. ReleasedOct 9, 2026v2.1

    The story and level pages in new art

    • The story has five new images in isometric pixel art – from the server hall where the Net awoke to the growing colony.
    • Every campaign level has its own image, and the help shows the buildings as they look on the map.
    • A tent camp for groups waiting for beds, and a new app icon.
  13. ReleasedOct 9, 2026v2.0

    The whole map in isometric pixel art

    Gravel roads, cracked asphalt and well-trodden paths. Carriers carry a lashed package in front of them and trucks drive with packages stacked on the bed.

  14. ReleasedOct 9, 2026v1.9–1.9.3

    Isometric view and new art

    • The map is shown at an angle from above. All buildings, ruins, forests, ground and people are redone in pixel art.
    • Workers dress for their trade, construction sites get scaffolding and fields grow in three stages.
    • A new splash screen and menu.
  15. ReleasedOct 8, 2026v1.8

    A deeper economy

    A tree nursery and forests that grow back, crop fields, stock targets, productivity per building and experience that makes workers faster.

  16. ReleasedOct 8, 2026v1.6–1.7.1

    Hills, valleys and living forests

    Elevation on the map, Swedish nature in double resolution, trees that fall and leave stumps, and paths that follow the direction people walk. Roads make people and robots faster.

  17. ReleasedOct 7–8, 2026v1.5–1.5.2

    The second act

    Four new campaign levels – the Ash Cloud, the Slag Hill, the Alliance and the Net's Core – guard robots and shareable map codes. The installed app asks when a new version is available.

  18. ReleasedOct 7, 2026v1.1–1.4.1

    Wind, roads and sound

    Wind and storms, natural movement, settlements in the ruins, roads built by the carriers, wall segments, wind power and batteries – and layered music that follows day, night and threat.

  19. ReleasedOct 6, 2026v1.0

    The first version

    Askfält becomes an installable web app that works offline, with a campaign, free play and three save slots. Before that: versions 0.1–0.19 with production chains, the campaign, night and rest, morale, trade and the new renderer.
