Licensing and credits

How the game’s artwork was made and which third-party software is used.

Artwork and animation

Askfält’s artwork and animations were created specifically for the game, mainly with the AI-based tool PixelLab.ai. The material has been selected, edited and assembled into the game’s world. No pre-made asset packs have been used.

The use of generative tools does not make the material free to use. Which rights apply depends on applicable law and the tools’ terms of use. The material is not licensed for redistribution.
